This record captures the physical QA findings selected for the immediate corrective release. It is deliberately bounded. Items outside this record are not automatically in scope.

## Home and general navigation

1. **FIX — Home artwork focus containment.** Artwork can protrude outside the cyan focus box/rounded corners. The focus container must fully contain artwork.
2. **FIX — Home horizontal row clipping.** Remove the artificial right-side clipping boundary. Rows should behave as continuous horizontal strips and may show the next card partially at the screen edge.
3. **FIX / corrective replication — Home row Move / Hide controls.** Replicate the approved Shield-observed Move and Hide row behaviour.
4. **HIGH-PRIORITY FIX — Continue Watching → playback → Back focus restoration.** Return must visibly restore the exact launching Continue Watching card and make directional navigation work immediately. Avoid the current state where no card is visibly focused and Down jumps to top navigation.
5. **FIX — Clock typography/size.** Align clock font/scale with intended top-navigation typography.
6. **FIX — Top blur/fade region.** Network & Files, Settings and Search currently blur/fade too deeply. Align their region with the successful Home/Movies/TV behaviour.

## Movies and TV library

7. **FIX — Vertical focused-row positioning.** Keep the focused grid row around the screen centre rather than pinned to top/bottom.
8. **FIX — Control-bar vertical alignment.** Bring Filters / Sort / Order / view / Columns controls down to align visually with the divider.
9. **HIGH-PRIORITY FIX — Library → Details → Back exact focus restoration.** Restore the exact selected card with immediately usable focus. Share deterministic focus-restoration logic with other return paths where appropriate.

## Details

10. **FIX — Details sub-content loading latency.** Investigate source/cache/render path for slow More Like This / Extras / lower-detail population; do not cosmetically mask it.
11. **FIX — Hero metadata formatting.** Correct year/runtime/rating alignment and formatting.
12. **FIX — Primary action containers.** Play/Resume, provider action and More should have permanent button containers, with focus treatment layered on top.
13. **FIX — Lower Details navigation/collapse behaviour.** Preserve liked hero-collapse behaviour. While collapsed, Up from Cast/lower content should reach the lower tab strip without forcing the full hero to expand.
14. **FIX — Compact information rows.** Use compact label/value rows such as `Runtime    1h 42m` rather than tall stacked labels/values.
15. **FIX / corrective redesign — Cast & Crew layout.** Keep Cast & Crew but make it substantially more compact. Exact replacement visual is **NEEDS DECISION**; do not invent it.

18. **FIX — Typing retains keyboard focus.** Selecting a letter must not jump focus back to the search box.
19. **FIX — Result posters.** Matching candidates should show relevant poster artwork.

## Playback HUD — approved corrective direction

26. **APPROVED — Remove hidden-HUD Up → More shortcut.** Hidden-HUD D-pad interaction should reveal the normal HUD/seek interface consistently without accidental seeking/navigation.
27. **APPROVED — Remove Info from the primary HUD.** Do not simply move it into More unless separately approved later.
28. **APPROVED — Four primary controls:** **Subtitles · Audio · Play/Pause · More**. Play/Pause is physically centred; do not invent a fifth control for symmetry. Exact pixel spacing is not specified.
29. **APPROVED — Remove links from HUD panels to full application Settings.** Playback-specific controls may remain within HUD panels; full app configuration belongs in Settings.
30. **DIRECTION — Simplify Subtitles hierarchy.** First level should emphasise current/off/available tracks plus Download Subtitles; less-frequent timing/appearance may live under Subtitle Options. Exact final menu remains under review.
31. **DIRECTION — Simplify Audio hierarchy.** First level should primarily select audio tracks; less-frequent playback-specific controls may live under Audio Options. Exact final menu remains under review.
32. **DIRECTION / NEEDS DECISION — More menu.** Remove Supernova Settings. Playback Speed / Play Mode / Format may remain for now. Report a Problem may be useful for contextual diagnostics, but the exact final contents are not yet approved.
