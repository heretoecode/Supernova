## Milestone policy
1. Approved — behaviour and visual decision settled, no release chosen.
2. Scheduled — owner explicitly chooses release.
3. In Development — implementation underway on identified branch.
4. Implemented — Awaiting Verification — code/build produced, owner QA outstanding.
5. Completed & Verified — installed/tested/accepted; record version, commit and evidence.

Do not duplicate old approved features in the future queue. Record residual fixes against their actual release handover.
